      How the system works

      Attendance is taken inside the hall over Bluetooth, with no internet and no projector. Each phone keeps a copy it cannot edit. When internet comes back, the records upload and the report updates itself.

      Once1. RegisterStudent enters matric number. It is locked to that phone.
      In class · offline2. OpenLecturer taps Start attendance. Phone shares course and session code by Bluetooth.
      In class · offline3. MarkStudents nearby tap Mark me present and confirm with fingerprint.
      In class · offline4. CloseLecturer marks dead-phone students by hand, then ends the session. No more marking.
      Online later5. UploadLecturer's and students' phones upload their copies when there is internet.
      Online later6. ReportPresent, absent, percentages and exam eligibility. Sent to the portal.

      Lecturer

      1. Opens the app and taps Start attendance for the course.
      2. Carries on teaching while names come in. A full hall takes 2–3 minutes.
      3. Marks students with a dead phone or no smartphone by hand.
      4. Taps End session. Nobody can mark after this.
      5. Sees the full report once the phone gets internet.

      Student

      1. Registers once with their matric number. It is locked to that phone.
      2. In class, the app shows Attendance is open by itself.
      3. Taps Mark me present and confirms with fingerprint.
      4. Sees their attendance percentage and gets a warning before falling below 75%.

      Staff

      1. Taps Clock in when they arrive at the office. Only works near the office beacon.
      2. Taps Clock out when leaving.
      3. The HOD sees who resumed and when. Registry gets monthly reports.

      How cheating is stopped

      TrickWhat stops it
      Marking from the hostelBluetooth only reaches about 10–30 m. Out of range, the app never shows the Mark button.
      Registering a friend's matric on your phoneEach matric number can be locked to one phone only. A second phone is refused.
      Carrying a friend's phone to class and marking for themEvery mark needs the owner's fingerprint or face. A different finger fails.
      Marking after the class endsWhen the lecturer ends the session, the Bluetooth signal stops and the session code stops working.
      Editing records before uploadRecords are signed on the phone. Any change breaks the signature and the server refuses it.

      Things people will ask

      • "I lost my phone." The student requests a phone change in the app. The course adviser or ICT unit approves it, and the old phone stops working.
      • "My phone is dead." The lecturer marks the student by hand. The report shows it was marked by hand.
      • "I don't have a smartphone." Same as above. The system never blocks a student from being marked.
      • "The lecturer's phone got stolen before upload." Every student's phone also holds a copy of its own record and uploads it separately.

      What the real system needs

      • An Android app for students and lecturers, because Bluetooth needs a native app. Most students use Android.
      • A website for HODs, Registry and admins to view reports and approve phone changes.
      • Portal access from the ICT unit, through its API or an export file.
      • Data privacy. Matric numbers and phone IDs are personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Fingerprints never leave the phone; the app only learns "matched" or "did not match".
